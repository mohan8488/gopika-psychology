Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of the questions people commonly ask before starting therapy.

How long is a therapy session?

Most sessions last approximately 50 minutes.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality is a fundamental part of psychological practice, except where legal obligations require otherwise.

Who can book a session?

All sessions take place online, by secure video. {{ site.Data.site.practice.notice }}

What if I need urgent help?

Online sessions are not suitable for emergencies. If you are in crisis or at risk of harm, please contact local emergency services straight away: in the UAE call 999 (police) or 998 (ambulance); in India call 112, or Tele-MANAS on 14416 for free mental health support.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on your goals and individual circumstances. We'll discuss this together during the initial consultation.
